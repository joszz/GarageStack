A self-hosted dashboard, trip log, charge history and map for your MG.

GarageStack signs in to the SAIC iSmart API, the same backend the official MG app uses, and turns what your car reports into something worth opening. It runs in Docker on your own hardware, with no subscription and no account beyond your MG login.

Get started View on GitHub

Works with modern MG cars

The ones built by SAIC Motor that use the MG iSmart app, such as the MG4, MG5, ZS EV and HS (Hybrid+ and PHEV). Hybrid, plug-in hybrid and full electric cars each get the cards that fit them. Classic British-built MGs (MGB, Midget, MGF and the like) are not supported.

Everything your car reports, in one place

More than the official app shows, and kept for as long as you like.

  • See the car at a glance

    Fuel, battery, tyre pressures, doors, windows and climate on one live dashboard, with cards you can reorder and hide.

  • Relive every drive

    Trips are saved automatically, named by where they went and drawn along the roads you took, with heatmaps and the speed limits on the way.

  • A trip log for the taxman

    Mark trips as business, commute or private, add notes, and export a month or a year as a spreadsheet.

  • Charge history

    Completed and ongoing sessions with their duration, charging type, power curve and state of charge.

  • Control it from anywhere

    Pre-condition the cabin, lock the car or flash its lights to find it, and see whether the car actually carried the command out.

  • Warm when you leave

    Schedule climate for weekday mornings or a one-off trip, with seat heating, the defrosters, and a skip for mild days.

  • Hear when something is off

    Push notifications for low tyre pressure, a car left unlocked, a window left open, charging complete and more.

  • An app on your phone

    Install it as an app, and it still opens without a connection, showing what it last loaded, maps included.

  • Keep it yours

    Everything runs on your own hardware, as one container or a Compose stack. Your data never goes to a cloud of ours, because there is none.

Fits into your homelab

Built to sit next to the rest of your self-hosted services.

  • Home Assistant The car appears through MQTT discovery, sharing GarageStack's MG session.
  • Homepage widget Key stats on your gethomepage.dev dashboard.
  • Single sign-on Authentik, Authelia, Keycloak, Pocket ID or any OpenID Connect provider.
  • Docker, amd64 and arm64 One all-in-one container or a Compose stack, on a PC, NAS or Raspberry Pi.
  • Unraid A template in Community Apps, with every setting in the template UI.
  • Read-only API Tokens for scripts and dashboards, and an OpenAPI description of every endpoint.
  • Calendar feed Maintenance, the Dutch APK and climate schedules in your calendar app.
  • What leaves your network Every outside service it talks to, and how to switch each one off.

Up and running in one command

The all-in-one image bundles everything it needs: the web app, PostgreSQL, the MQTT broker and the SAIC gateway.

One session per MG account. Signing in to the official MG app with the same account signs GarageStack out. Give the app a secondary account and keep the owner account for GarageStack: how to set that up.

Docker 
docker run -d \
  --name garagestack \
  -p 8080:80 \
  -v ./garagestack-data:/data \
  -e SAIC_USER=your@email.com \
  -e SAIC_PASSWORD=yourpassword \
  -e SAIC_REGION=eu \
  -e CORS_ORIGIN=http://192.168.1.100:8080 \
  ghcr.io/joszz/garagestack:latest

Then open port 8080 and sign in with your MG login. SAIC_REGION is eu, au, tr or br, and CORS_ORIGIN is the address you open in your browser.
