Your MG, on your own server.

A self-hosted dashboard, trip log, charge history and map for modern MG cars. No subscription, and no account beyond your MG login.

Get started See what it does

MIT licence Docker, amd64 and arm64

For MG cars built by SAIC Motor that use the MG iSmart app, such as the MG4, MG5, ZS EV and HS. Hybrid, plug-in hybrid and full electric.

Everything your car reports, in one place

More than the official app shows, and kept for as long as you like.

  • Live dashboard Fuel, battery, tyres, doors, windows and climate at a glance.
  • Every drive on a map Saved by itself, snapped to the roads, against the speed limit.
  • Trip log Business, commute or private, exported for the taxman.
  • Charging and energy Every charge with its power curve, and years of statistics.
  • Remote control Climate, locks and charging, now or on a schedule.
  • Notifications Low tyres, a car left unlocked, a finished charge and more.
